About

We’ve sat on the other side of the table.

Before Rovell, we spent six years inside bridging and development lenders, working beside credit committees deciding what deals got funded and why. Now we use that knowledge for our clients. We prepare each case the way a credit committee wants to see it, and take it to the lenders most likely to say yes.

  1. 01

    Prepared before it’s submitted.

    Every case is packaged with the answers a lender will ask for, so it moves faster through credit.

  2. 02

    Transparent from the outset.

    We give you an honest view of a scheme’s prospects from the start, including what may need to change to secure funding.

  3. 03

    Aligned with your outcome.

    We charge no upfront broker fee. Our remuneration comes from the lender, on completion of your facility.

Rovell Finance Limited

Rovell Finance Limited is an independent commercial finance brokerage, not a lender; as such we can introduce you to a wide range of finance providers depending on your requirements and circumstances. We are not independent financial advisors and so are unable to provide you with independent financial advice. Rovell Finance Limited will receive payment(s) in the form of commission from the finance provider if you decide to enter into an agreement with them. We work with both discretionary and non-discretionary commission models. Commission payments are factored into the interest rate you pay.
