How it works

Our Process

01

A call

Fifteen minutes. What you are buying or building, what it is worth, and how you intend to repay it. Usually enough to know whether this is worth pursuing.

02

Indicative terms

Real numbers from real lenders, rate, fees, term and what they will need. Normally back with you inside 24 to 48 hours.

03

Application & valuation

I package the case properly the first time, so underwriting is not spent chasing documents. You get told what is happening without asking.

04

Legals & drawdown

Solicitors instructed, conditions cleared, funds released. I stay on it through to completion, and afterwards if the exit needs arranging.
