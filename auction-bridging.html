Why bridging

Mortgage vs bridging at auction.

🐌

Standard mortgage

Slow and picky

  • ⏱️Often 6+ weeks to complete
  • 🚫Often refuses properties that need work
  • 📉Short lease or unusual title? Often declined
  • 📅Long-term finance
VS
Built for auction⚡

Bridging loan

Fast and flexible

  • 🚀Can complete in days
  • 🛠️Properties needing work often considered
  • 🧩Unusual titles and leases assessed case by case
  • 🔁Short term, then refinance or sell

Bridging is short-term finance and usually costs more than a mortgage. We plan your exit (refinance or sale) before you borrow.

Questions

Quick answers.

Can I arrange finance before I bid?

Yes, and it is the best time. You know your budget and you bid with confidence.

Can bridging complete in 28 days?

Often, but it depends on the property, the lender and the solicitors. Nothing is guaranteed, which is why starting early matters.

What if the property cannot get a mortgage?

Bridging lenders focus on the security and your exit plan, so many properties that fail a mortgage valuation can still be funded.

What does it cost?

Rates and fees depend on the deal. Send me the lot and I will give you a clear picture of the costs before you commit.

What happens if I miss completion?

You can lose your deposit and may be liable for losses on a resale. Check your auction legal pack for the exact terms.

Bidding soon or just won? Let's talk.

The sooner I know the lot and the completion date, the more options you have.
